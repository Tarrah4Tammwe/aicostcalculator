An independent pricing desk for model APIs.

AI Cost Calculator helps developers, founders, and product managers turn a workload into a monthly figure before they commit to a provider. It is published by Tarrah and it is not affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, or DeepSeek.

What the site is for

List prices are scattered across five documentation sites, and they move. A single sticker price also hides the thing that usually dominates a bill: how many tokens you write, how much of the prompt repeats, and whether a long prompt crosses a cliff. The calculator puts one workload on every current row. The rate card keeps the source next to the number. The guide explains the vocabulary, and the methodology records the formula and the exclusions.

How a model gets on the card

A row is added when the provider publishes a text token price for a model you can buy now. The check date on the page is the day those pages were read. Retired prices stay out of the estimator so the default choice cannot be a model the provider has already replaced. When a launch note and a general rule disagree, the explicit number on the launch note is the one we use. That is why Claude Opus 5.5's cache read is $0.20, the figure on Anthropic's announcement, rather than a flat 10% of input.

What we do not do

We do not sell API access, resell credits, or take a commission when you open a provider's pricing page. Links to those pages are citations. We do not rank models by quality. A cheaper row is a cheaper row, and the product decision is still yours. We do not store calculator inputs on a server. The privacy policy describes the advertising cookies that can be set when an ad is shown.

Advertising

The site is applying to run ads so the reference can stay free. Ads are labeled, they sit after the tool and the explanation, and the policy pages are not used as ad inventory. If an ad appears to be part of the calculator, that is a mistake. Write to us and we will move it.

Reach the publisher

Pricing corrections, privacy requests, and general notes go to sheblackfounded@gmail.com. The contact page lists what to include so a correction can be checked against the source.
