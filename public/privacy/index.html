Privacy policy

Last updated 26 September 2026. This policy covers aicostcalculator.dev, published by Tarrah. Questions go to sheblackfounded@gmail.com.

The calculator

Estimates are computed in your browser. We do not receive the request counts or token counts you type, and there is no account to log into. The page writes those figures into the address bar so you can refresh, bookmark, or share a workload. If you share the link, you share those numbers. They describe a volume of tokens, not your name or a payment method.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, passwords, or payment details.
  • No server-side copy of calculator inputs.
  • No sale of personal information.

The host, Vercel, receives ordinary server logs when your browser requests a page, such as IP address, user agent, and the URL. Those logs are used to operate and secure the site.

Advertising

Pages with a tool or a reference article may show ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners can use cookies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on previous visits to this site and others. You can review and limit ad personalization at Google ad settings, and you can read Google's description at how Google uses advertising cookies.

The calculator, the rate card, and the guides are usable if you block those cookies. Ads may then be generic, or absent.

Email you send us

If you write to sheblackfounded@gmail.com, we use the message to answer you and, for a pricing correction, to check the provider page. We do not add that address to a marketing list.

Your choices

You can use the rate card and the guide with JavaScript disabled. You can block third-party cookies in your browser. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold, to correct it, or to delete it. Email the address above with the request. Because the calculator does not store your inputs, the information we can hold is limited to email you sent us and routine host logs.

Children

The site is a professional reference for API pricing. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Continued use of the site after that date means the new policy applies to later visits.
